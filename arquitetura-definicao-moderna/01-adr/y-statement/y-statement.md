# Y-Statement (Architectural Decision)

> Fonte: Olaf Zimmermann / ADR community — https://adr.github.io/adr-templates/

## Forma curta

In the context of `<use case/user story u>`,
facing `<concern c>`
we decided for `<option o>`
to achieve `<quality q>`,
accepting `<downside d>`.

## Forma longa

In the context of `<use case/user story u>`,
facing `<concern c>`,
we decided for `<option o>`
and neglected `<other options>`,
to achieve `<quality q>`,
accepting `<downside d>`,
because `<additional rationale>`.

## Uso

- Decisão rápida em 1 parágrafo (PR, design review, chat).
- Complementa ADR completo (MADR/Nygard); não substitui quando há várias opções com trade-offs.
