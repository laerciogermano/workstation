# Fansly — IA (política · produto · promessa)

Inspeção 2026-11-10 (CDP `fansly` :9266 — público + Help Center + ToS + conta fan).

## Política

Help Center · [AI Generated Content on Fansly](https://help.fansly.com/en/articles/12315578-ai-generated-content-on-fansly) (8 jul 2026) + ToS:

- **Photorealistic / lifelike AI is not allowed.** Labeling as AI-generated **does not** make it permissible.
- Ban: (i) photorealistic/lifelike content substantially generated/created by AI (deepfakes, face-swap, synthetic “real humans”); (ii) AI systems that **directly interact with Users** to generate personalized responses/imagery/experiences **without human oversight**; (iii) misrepresenting AI material as authentic human performance/creation.
- Virtual / non-photorealistic creators: allowed if registered with **real legal identity** + standard ID verification; co-performers (even voice-only virtual) also need verification.
- AI depicting minors in sexual/suggestive contexts: strictly prohibited.
- NCII help: deepfakes / AI-generated forgeries reportáveis como digital forgery.

## Produto

- **Sem suite GenAI** nativa (sem chatbot creator, voice agent, geração de mídia, messaging AI).
- Pitch = discovery + media/social + monetização humana; anti-deepfake / “conteúdo humano real”.

## Promessa

- Help + ToS = enforcement anti-fotorealista e anti-bot de chat.
- Marketing landing: Sign up / Explore / Become A Creator — **zero** “AI creator” no pitch.

## UX (onde aparece)

- Help article dedicado + cláusula ToS.
- NCII reporting menciona deepfake/AI.
- Não aparece no signup/home logada como feature positiva.
