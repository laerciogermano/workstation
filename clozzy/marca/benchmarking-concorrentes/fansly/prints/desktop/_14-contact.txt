Cancellation Policy
Cancel your subscription plan any time with these steps:
1. Login to your account.
2. Go to your subscriptions page. You can see the creators you are subscribed to.
3. Go to the creators profile and press cancel subscription.
4. Continue through the confirmation message.
If you cancel around your usual billing date, your next payment may have already been taken.
If your subscription page shows a date when your subscription will end, you’ve successfully cancelled and won’t be charged again. If it doesn’t show this, try the cancel steps again making sure you reach the confirmation message.
