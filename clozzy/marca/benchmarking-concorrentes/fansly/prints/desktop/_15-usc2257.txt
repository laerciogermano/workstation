Section 2257 Disclosure Statement

Content Subject to Section 2257: All models, actors, actresses, and other persons that appear in any visual portrayal of actual or simulated sexually explicit conduct appearing on, or otherwise contained on Fansly were over the age of eighteen (18) years at the time the visual image was produced. Records required for all depictions of actual or simulated sexually explicit conduct as defined by Section 2257 are on file with the appropriate Records Custodian.

Exemption Statement: All other visual depictions displayed on Fansly are exempt from the provision of Section 2257 because: 1) they do not portray conduct as specifically listed in 18 U.S.C §2256 (2)(A) (i) through (iv); 2) they do not portray conduct as specifically listed in 18 U.S.C. §2257A produced after March 19, 2009; 3) they do not portray conduct listed in 18 U.S.C. §2256(2)(A)(v) produced after March 19, 2009; 4) the visual depictions were created prior to July 3, 1995; or, 5) Fansly does not act as a “producer” with respect to the dissemination of such exempt images as that term is defined in 28 C.F.R. §75.1(c).

The title of this work is: Fansly.
