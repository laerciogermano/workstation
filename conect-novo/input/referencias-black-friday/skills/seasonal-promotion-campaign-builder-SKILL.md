# Seasonal Promotion Campaign Builder

Fonte: https://mnaitools.com/blog/seasonal-promotion-campaign-builder-1793629901725 (SKILL.md parcial publicado; steps 1–5 completos no artigo; step 6 truncado na fonte).

## Trigger

Use when the owner wants to plan and execute a promotional campaign tied to a holiday, season, or calendar event (e.g., Black Friday, summer sale, back-to-school, Valentine's Day).

## Input

- Business type and products or services offered
- Target season or specific holiday/date
- Promotion offer (e.g., 20% off, free shipping, BOGO)
- Target customer description
- Campaign duration in days (default 7)

## Steps

1. Confirm the promotion details and target audience from the input.
2. Create a campaign countdown timeline with key send dates (e.g., teaser 7 days out, main launch 3 days out, last-chance 1 day out).
3. Write a 3-email sequence:
   - Teaser email: subject line + 3-sentence body building anticipation
   - Launch email: subject line + 5-sentence body with the full offer
   - Last-chance email: subject line + 3-sentence urgency body
4. Write 3 social media captions (Instagram/Facebook) matched to the same timeline.
5. Provide one SMS/text message draft (under 160 characters) for the launch day.
