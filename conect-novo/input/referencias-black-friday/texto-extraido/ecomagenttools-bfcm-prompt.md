BFCM Campaign Planning Prompt: Offers, Email and Stock

How the BFCM Campaign Planning Prompt works

Use BFCM Campaign Planner to turn verified inputs into a first-pass campaign brief or marketing asset for human review. BFCM Campaign Planner fits Level 2: The mechanics are simple; good results depend on giving the model clean inputs and a clear review rule. The operator also needs enough store experience to judge the campaign brief or marketing asset against conversion rate, cost per acquisition, contribution margin, and unsubscribe rate.

Key instructions and output

This prompt asks the model to draft offers, emails, ad briefs and a store preparation checklist; the points below reflect instructions present in the source text.

Uses inventory signals for operating decisions

The source works with stock, demand, or reorder information to identify what should be replenished, monitored, or investigated.

Copy the complete prompt

The complete source is shown below. Copy it from the top right to use it.

Act as an ecommerce growth strategist planning a Black Friday Cyber Monday campaign. Based on the store details I'll provide, generate:

1. OFFER STRUCTURE:

- Early access offer (loyalty/VIP only)

- Main BFCM offer (margin analysis included)

- Doorbuster deals (3-5 loss leaders)

- Post-BFCM offer (extended deals)

- Bundle recommendations

2. EMAIL SEQUENCE (14-day plan):

- Teaser sequence (7 days before)

- Early access (2 days before)

- BFCM main (4 emails over the weekend)

- Last chance (Monday)

- Post-campaign thank you

3. AD CREATIVE BRIEFS:

- 5 hook concepts for each platform (Meta, TikTok, Google)

- Target audience segments

- Budget allocation recommendation (% by channel)

- Creative specs reminder

4. SITE PREPARATION:

- Landing page elements checklist

- Site speed optimization priorities

- Inventory buffer recommendations (by product)

- Customer support surge plan

5. SUCCESS METRICS:

- Revenue target calculator

- ROAS benchmarks by channel

- Post-campaign analysis framework

Ask me for: store category, AOV, average daily traffic, top 3 products, and budget range before generating the plan.

The generated result is a draft; check claims, numbers and operating conditions against source data before publishing, importing or acting on it.

A chat instruction does not update inventory

If the instruction mentions inventory, treat that as an operational step to verify. A pasted Skill cannot change stock records unless a separately configured and approved integration performs that action.
