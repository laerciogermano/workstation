Features that turn attendees into participants

Create excitement before the event begins

Cvent Attendee Hub encourages attendees to start interacting with the event the moment they have registered. Create initial engagement points that create buzz and identify attendee expectations before the opening session.

  • Personal agenda building & appointment scheduling so attendees can make the most out of their time at your event
  • Highlight content such as new confirmed speakers, popular sessions and sponsors
  • Hold live discussions groups and spark the conversations between attendees
  • Collect pre-event feedback with surveys and learn what attendees are expecting from your event
  • Display teaser videos and know-before-you-go tips to ensure attendees are excited and prepared

     

Maximum engagement and flawless event execution

Keep your attendees engaged and keep your program on track.

  • Gamification adds exciting attendee challenges to the event experience
  • Session engagement helps audiences participate through polls, surveys, and Q&A
  • Up-level session videos with the power of Cvent Studio
  • Push notifications and announcements help attendees keep informed of important events and last minute changes
  • Activity feeds showcase the fun with photo uploads and attendees posts
  • Ensure sponsor value with dozens of activation opportunities
  • Digital event experience for attendees at home, at the hotel, or on the show floor

Build a community after the event

While the event may end, the conversations don’t have to. Keep your attendees engaged long after the event with your content and with each other.

  • Collect post-event feedback with surveys and learn what worked and what didn’t
  • Attendees can tie up loose ends with document downloads and 1:1 messaging
  • Video on demand means that attendees won’t ever miss a session
  • Discussion groups let the conversation continue long after the event
  • Post-event analytics can tell you how engaged your attendees were and how well your event performed
  • Upload event content online with Events+ and use it to market your next event

FAQs

Cvent Attendee Hub is an engagement platform (web + mobile) that connects attendees to your event’s content, networking, and sponsors before, during, and after the event, across in-person, virtual, and hybrid formats. 

It improves the experience by centralizing agendas, video, interactions, and exhibitor content in one place and layering in AI, appointments, and analytics so every attendee journey feels intentional and connected. 

Yes. Attendees can build and manage their personal agendas and schedule 1:1 appointments with other attendees, sponsors, and exhibitors directly in Attendee Hub and the Event App, so meetings and sessions live in a single, unified schedule.

Attendee Hub includes chat, Q&A, polls, surveys, discussions, and gamification to turn attendees from passive viewers into active participants across web and mobile. 

Features like activity feeds, reactions, and leaderboards further amplify interaction and keep energy high during the event. 

Attendee Hub delivers a digital event experience for people onsite and remote, with live/ simulive streaming, on-demand content, networking, and engagement tools all accessible via web and the Event App. 

Onsite attendees get mobile access to agendas and engagement, while virtual attendees join sessions, visit booths, and network through the web interface using the same underlying platform. 

A standard event app is typically a mobile agenda and info hub; Attendee Hub is a full engagement layer spanning web and mobile that adds broadcast-quality video, AI-powered networking and recommendations, appointments, virtual expo, and deep engagement analytics across the event lifecycle. 

It’s designed to support in-person, virtual, and hybrid events holistically rather than just providing an onsite companion app.
