Frequently asked questions about attendee engagement

"We saw an increase in forty two percent [from previous year] on the number of meetings that people were organizing through the app."
Sharon Roessen

COO | Terrapinn

Why is attendee engagement important for B2B events?

Engaged attendees are more likely to return, connect with exhibitors, participate in sessions, and provide feedback. Strong engagement drives measurable ROI for organizers, sponsors, and participants.

How can technology improve attendee engagement?

Technology streamlines the attendee journey—from discovery and personalization to networking and feedback. Tools like mobile apps, matchmaking, and live interaction features create a more connected, immersive experience.

What are the key metrics to measure attendee engagement?

Common engagement KPIs include session participation rates, meeting volume, networking connections, chat activity, poll/Q&A responses, and content consumption. Platforms like Swapcard provide real-time tracking and post-event analytics.

What’s the difference between engagement and attendance?

Attendance measures how many people show up. Engagement measures how actively those people participate, through meetings, polls, chat, and content interactions. Engagement is what drives real value from the event.

Do attendees need to download the app to engage?

Swapcard is available on both mobile and web. While the mobile app offers an enhanced experience, attendees can fully engage via browser, before, during, and after the event.

Can organizers control who attendees can message or meet?

Meeting rules and messaging permissions can be set based on attendee type, ticket, or role.

Does Swapcard support virtual and hybrid events?

Swapcard supports all formats—in‑person, virtual, and hybrid—with seamless engagement tools that adapt to each context.

Can attendees join group meetings or only 1:1?

Swapcard supports both 1:1 and group meetings (up to 10 participants), depending on event configuration.

How does the AI know what to recommend?

Swapcard's AI uses behavior-based data (clicks, views, bookmarks, profile interests) to suggest relevant people, sessions, and content. It continuously learns and improves throughout the event.

How do organizers track and report on engagement?

Real‑time dashboards and post‑event reports show engagement across sessions, networking, messaging, and content, ROI to sponsors and stakeholders.
