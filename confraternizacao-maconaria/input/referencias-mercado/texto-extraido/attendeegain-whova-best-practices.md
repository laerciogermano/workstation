 Whova Event App: Setup, Tips & Best Practice Guide 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 Event Marketing 
 
 

 
 
 May 19, 2026 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 Back to Blog 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 In the rapidly evolving landscape of event technology, Whova has consistently maintained its position as one of the most beloved and highly adopted mobile event apps on the market. Renowned for its incredible attendee engagement tools, robust networking features, and seamless community-building capabilities, it is a favorite for conferences, trade shows, and academic symposiums.

 However, simply purchasing a Whova license does not guarantee a 100% adoption rate or a buzzing community board. Like any powerful tool, its success depends entirely on how it is configured and deployed. A hastily set up app will be ignored by attendees, while a strategically structured app becomes the digital heartbeat of your event.

 If you want to move beyond the basics and unlock the platform’s true potential, this comprehensive Whova event app setup guide will walk you through the essential phases, expert tips, and industry best practices to ensure your 2026 event is an interactive masterpiece.

 
 
 
 
 Phase 1: The Core Build (Information & Aesthetics)

 
 
 The foundational phase of your Whova event app setup is all about ensuring attendees have zero friction when trying to find what they need. If the schedule is confusing or the branding looks off, attendees will abandon the app on day one.

 
 
 1. Build a Dynamic, Interactive Agenda

 
 
 Do not just upload a static PDF. Use Whova’s native agenda builder to create an interactive schedule.

 Pro Tip: Color-code your sessions by “Tracks” (e.g., Marketing, IT, Leadership) or by ticket type. This allows attendees to easily filter the massive schedule and add specific sessions to their “Personal Agenda.”

 Link Everything: Ensure every session is linked directly to the respective speaker’s profile and any downloadable PDF handouts or presentation slides.

 
 
 2. Complete Speaker Profiles

 
 
 Your speakers are a major draw. A blank speaker profile with just a name looks unprofessional.

 The “Speaker Form” Feature: Whova has a brilliant feature that allows you to send an automated link directly to your speakers, letting them upload their own high-resolution headshots, bios, and social media links. This saves your team hours of manual data entry and ensures the app looks fully populated.

 
 
 3. Brand Customization

 
 
 Whova allows you to inject your brand’s DNA into the app interface. Upload your official event logo, set your primary brand colors for the app’s headers and buttons, and customize the banner images. A cohesive, white-labeled feel builds trust with your corporate audience.

 
 
 Phase 2: Supercharging Attendee Engagement

 
 
 Whova’s biggest differentiator is its engagement suite. If you are not using these features, you are wasting the platform. Here is how to set them up for maximum impact.

 
 
 1. The Community Board (The Digital Watercooler)

 
 
 The Community Board is where the magic happens. Do not wait for attendees to start conversations—seed them yourself during the setup phase.

 Best Practice: Create customized discussion topics a week before the event. Add topics like “Introduce Yourself,” “First-Time Attendees Q&A,” and “Dinner Meetups in [City Name].”

 Article Sharing: Post relevant industry articles to spark debate before the keynote speaker even takes the stage.

 
 
 2. Live Polling and Q&A

 
 
 Set up your session Q&A and Live Polls in advance.

 Speaker Coordination: Work with your speakers to create 2 or 3 polls per session. For example, “What is your biggest marketing challenge right now?” Deploying a poll 5 minutes into a session guarantees attendees open the Whova app, making them highly likely to submit Q&A questions later in the session

 
 
 3. Gamification and Leaderboards

 
 
 People love to compete. Turn on Whova’s Gamification feature to incentivize app adoption.

 How it works: Assign points for desired behaviors (e.g., 100 points for filling out the post-session survey, 50 points for posting a photo, 20 points for asking a question).

 The Prize: Announce via a push notification that the top 3 people on the Leaderboard at the end of the event will win a major prize (like a gift card or free tickets for next year). Watch your engagement metrics skyrocket.

 
 
 
 
 Phase 3: Facilitating Elite Networking

 
 
 In 2026, professionals attend conferences to network just as much as they do for the content. Your Whova event app setup should make connecting effortless.

 
 
 1. Enable AI Matchmaking

 
 
 Whova’s AI algorithm acts as a digital concierge. During the setup, ensure you require attendees to fill out their industry, job role, and interests during registration. Whova will use this data to automatically suggest high-value connections (e.g., matching a startup founder with an investor).

 
 
 2. Profile QR Codes & Digital Business Cards

 
 
 Remind attendees via an opening-day announcement to use the app’s built-in QR code scanner. This replaces physical business cards. When an attendee scans another’s badge, their contact info is instantly saved to their phone, and they can easily export these contacts to their CRM post-event.

 
 
 3. Virtual Meet-ups and Roundtables

 
 
 If you have a hybrid component, use Whova to schedule dedicated virtual video roundtables. Attendees can drop into these video rooms directly from the app to discuss niche topics, bridging the gap between physical and remote participants.

 
 
 Phase 4: Delivering Massive Value to Sponsors

 
 
 Sponsors pay the bills, and they expect measurable ROI. Whova provides excellent tools to make your sponsors very happy, provided you set them up correctly.

 
 
 1. Build Interactive Digital Booths

 
 
 Do not just upload a sponsor’s logo and call it a day. Utilize Whova’s tiered exhibitor profiles.

 Add Rich Media: Upload their promotional videos, PDF brochures, and special coupon codes directly into their digital booth.

 Lead Generation Button: Ensure the “Request Info” or “Drop a Business Card” button is highly visible so attendees can express interest with a single tap.

 
 
 2. The Sponsor Passport Contest

 
 
 This is a game-changer for driving physical foot traffic. Set up a “Passport Contest” within the app. Attendees must visit a specific number of physical sponsor booths and scan a unique QR code located at the booth to receive a “stamp” in their digital app passport. Completing the passport enters them into a grand prize draw.

 
 
 
 
 Best Practices: Launching and Managing the App

 
 
 A great setup is only half the battle. How you deploy the app dictates your adoption rate.

 The 2-Week Launch Window: Do not launch the app the day before the event. Send the download link via email exactly 14 days prior. This gives attendees time to build their schedules, update their profiles, and start using the community board.

 Push Notifications (Use Sparingly): Whova allows you to send instant push notifications to all attendees. Use this power wisely. Reserve it for critical updates (e.g., “Keynote starting in 10 minutes!” or “Room change for the Marketing track”). If you spam them, they will turn off notifications.

 The Onsite “App Help” Desk: Always have a dedicated staff member near the physical registration desk whose sole job is to help older or less tech-savvy attendees download the app, log in, and find their QR code for check-in.

 
 
 Conclusion

 
 
 Whova is not just a digital brochure; it is a catalyst for community, networking, and sponsor lead generation. By dedicating the time to execute a flawless Whova event app setup —from seeding the community boards to architecting gamified passport contests—you transform passive attendees into active participants. Follow these guidelines, and your event will not just be remembered for its great content, but for the incredible connections your technology facilitated.
