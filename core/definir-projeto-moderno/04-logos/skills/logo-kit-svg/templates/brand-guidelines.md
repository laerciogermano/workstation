# Brand guidelines (one-pager)

## Mark

- **Primary symbol:** `assets/icon.svg` (512×512)
- **README hero:** `assets/logo.svg` + `assets/logo-animated.svg`

## Color

All fills come from the application theme ([references/token-table.md](../references/token-table.md)). Do not invent hex values for UI surfaces.

## Clear space

Minimum padding around the squircle mark: 8% of icon width.

## Names

- **Brand name** (logo, boards, social, web): as in the brief.
- **Legal name** (only on formal print — letterhead legal line, invoices, contracts): as recorded in the brief with its source. Never inside the logo SVG, never invented.

## Lockup

- Secondary-word treatment (size, weight, tracking) follows the taste checkpoint recorded in the brief; reuse it across lockup, board typography panel, and letterhead instead of re-deciding per asset.

## Don't

- Trace marks from `library/` (study only).
- Ship wordmark-only banners without the 3-column blueprint.
- Pad boards or letterheads with invented contact details, slogans, or legal suffixes to look complete.
